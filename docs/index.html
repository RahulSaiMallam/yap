Press, talk, paste.

A free, open-source dictation app for macOS. Hold a key, speak, and your words land in any app. Everything runs locally — no cloud, no telemetry, no account.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Apple Silicon · macOS 14+ · ~14 MB

Hold Fn to talk. The pill appears at the bottom of your screen.

🔒

100% local

Whisper runs on your Mac. Audio never leaves your machine. No cloud, no logging, no telemetry.

⚡

Snappy

Decoding in 1–2 seconds for short phrases. Feels native.

🌍

Multilingual

30+ languages including Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Spanish, French, Mandarin, Japanese, Arabic, and more.

💬

Smart paste

Drops into the focused text field. In chat apps it auto-submits with a configurable delay.

✨

AI cleanup (optional)

Pipe transcripts through a local Ollama model to remove "um/uh", fix punctuation, and tighten the prose.

🎛️

Yours to tune

Hotkey, model, language, vocabulary, auto-submit delay — every value is exposed in Settings.

Install in 60 seconds

1

Download Yap.dmg

Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later.

2

Open the DMG and drag Yap to your Applications folder.

3

Right-click Yap in Applications, then choose Open.

Yap is signed but not notarized (no $99/yr Developer ID), so the first launch needs this manual step. Subsequent launches are normal.

4

Grant Microphone, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring when prompted.

Required for the global hotkey and for pasting into other apps.

5

Hold the Fn key, speak, release. Your words appear wherever your cursor is.
